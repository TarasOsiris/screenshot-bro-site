# Entity facts — Screenshot Bro

The canonical description of the product. **Copy from here verbatim** for every
directory listing, profile, and press mention.

Why verbatim matters: language models build their picture of a product by
aggregating how it is described across many independent sources. Twenty listings
that describe Screenshot Bro in the same words produce a confident, consistent
answer; twenty that each paraphrase produce a vague one. Consistency is the
asset — not creativity.

**Long (directory "about" fields)**
```
Screenshot Bro is a native Mac and iPad app for producing App Store and Google Play
screenshots. Instead of one design per size, the whole listing lives on a single
continuous canvas: every device size, every screenshot row, and every locale side by
side. Zoom out to see the entire set, zoom in to fix one headline.

It includes device frames for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android, text, shapes, gradients,
images and SVG, 35+ starter templates, 30 language presets with on-device
auto-translate and per-shape text, position, and image overrides, batch export to PNG
or JPEG organized by locale and row, and direct App Store Connect upload on Pro.
Project files are plain JSON, so they diff cleanly and belong to you.

The free tier has no signup and no expiry: 1 project, 3 rows, 5 templates per row,
every device frame, shape, and locale, and watermark-free exports. Pro lifts those
limits and adds App Store Connect upload and iCloud sync.

Made by Nineva Studios.
```

## Feature list (for checkbox-style directory fields)

- Native Mac and iPad app (macOS 15+, iPadOS 18+)
- App Store and Google Play screenshot sizes
- Device frames: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android
- Multi-row layouts with backgrounds spanning several screenshots
- 35+ starter templates
- Text, shapes, gradients, images, SVG
- 30 language presets plus custom locale codes
- On-device auto-translate with translation progress tracking
- Per-shape locale overrides (text, position, size, image)
- Batch export PNG/JPEG organized by locale and row
- Direct App Store Connect upload (Pro)
- iCloud sync (Pro)
- Plain-JSON project files — https://screenshotbro.app/docs/project-schema
- Watermark-free exports on the free tier

## Rules

- Never claim features we don't have. We do **not** have: Apple Watch / TV /
  Vision Pro sizes, an API, video export, a Windows or Linux version.
- Say "30 language presets plus custom codes", never "30+ languages" — the
  distinction is real and competitors with 80+ will correct us.
- Screenshots for listings: use the same set everywhere (see `public/` and the
  App Store listing) so the visual identity matches the text identity.
